Friday 20 November 2026 in one image.

declaration" from the Shenzhen trilateral (three flags, three leaders),
then the day's five most consequential stories, each with its own small

5. **Houthi missile hits Riyadh tower** — Saudi wealth fund HQ damaged

Ticker: Voyager 1 passes one light-day from Earth · Anthropic debuts
among the world's most valuable companies · Crimean Bridge hit a third

Every headline was checked against the reporting named above; nothing
is invented, and nothing is shown as decided before it happens (the
declaration says nothing about Ukraine or Iran, and the image says so;
the Iran decision is *pushed*, not taken). The page was compiled early
on 20 November 2026 (about 01:30 UTC), so it covers the news available
by then — the day was still in progress, and the image says so.

The image is drawn and typeset entirely with code (`compose.py`, PIL —
no generated media) and stored in this repository as news.jpg
(1024x1024 JPEG, 203,581 bytes, under the 1 MiB cap). The program only
copies those exact bytes to the output, so running it twice writes
identical bytes.
